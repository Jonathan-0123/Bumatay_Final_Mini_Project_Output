Safety tips

Short habits that make a big difference. Pick two or three and start this week.

Walking at night

  • Stay on lit, busy paths even if they are longer.
  • Share your live location with a friend until you arrive.
  • Keep one earbud out so you can hear what is around you.
  • Request a free escort from the Campus Security desk.

Around campus

Labs and library

Keep bags and laptops within sight. Never prop open a locked door for someone you do not know.

Dormitories

Lock your door even for a short trip down the hall. Report broken locks and lights the same day.

Bikes and belongings

Use a U-lock on the frame and wheel. Write your serial number somewhere safe in case you need to report a theft.

Online and on your phone

Protect your accounts

Use a different password for each account and turn on two-step sign-in for your student email.

Check the sender

Campus offices will never ask for your password by message. When in doubt, call the office directly.

Think before you post

Live posts about where you are can tell strangers where you are not. Share after you leave.

If something happens

  1. Get to a safe place. Move toward people, light, or a blue-light phone.
  2. Call for help. Dial 911 for danger, or the Campus Security desk for everything else.
  3. Write it down. Note the time, place, and what you saw while it is fresh.
  4. Tell us. Use the contact form so security can follow up.

In an emergency

Call 911

Then call the Campus Security desk at (02) 8000-0000.
