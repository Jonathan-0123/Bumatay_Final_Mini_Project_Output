Help on campus

Every service below is free for students. You do not need a reason to use them.

Blue-light phones

Blue-light phones stand on the main paths and at building entrances. Press the one large button and a security officer answers, sees your location, and sends help. You do not have to speak for help to be sent.

Test it once during daylight so it feels familiar.

Services

Campus Security desk

Open 24 hours. Reports, escorts, and lost keys.

(02) 8000-0000 · Main Gate Office

Night escort

A trained student marshal walks you to your door. Request at least 10 minutes ahead.

Daily 6 PM to 1 AM · Ext. 110

Health clinic

First aid, basic checkups, and referrals to partner hospitals.

Weekdays 8 AM to 5 PM · Ground floor, Student Center

Counseling office

Confidential talks with a licensed counselor about stress, safety, or anything on your mind.

Weekdays 9 AM to 4 PM · Book by email

Lost and found

Items are kept for 30 days. Bring your student ID to claim them.

Main Gate Office

Facilities repair

Report broken lights, locks, and gates so they are fixed quickly.

Ext. 120 · or use the contact form

In an emergency

Call 911

Then call the Campus Security desk at (02) 8000-0000.
