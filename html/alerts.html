Campus alerts

Know what an alert means before you ever receive one.

Hear the alert tone

Text description of the audio

A short chime of two rising notes, a lower note followed by a higher note. It repeats three times with a brief pause between each repeat. The whole recording lasts about six seconds.

Three alert levels

Advisory

Something to be aware of, such as a road closure or a power outage. No action needed beyond reading the message.

Watch

A situation that may affect you, such as a typhoon warning. Review your plan and follow updates.

Emergency

A threat is happening now. Follow the instructions in the message right away and call 911 if you are in danger.

Get alerts on your phone

Register your mobile number and student email through the Campus Security desk. Alerts arrive by text and email. Check that your details are current at the start of every term.
