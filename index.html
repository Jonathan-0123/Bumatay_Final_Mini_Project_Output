Safer together

Most campus incidents are quiet ones: a bike gone from the rack, a laptop left in a study room, a walk home that felt wrong. Reporting them early helps security spot patterns and fix the cause, such as a broken light.

If something feels off, trust that feeling and ask for help. You never need to wait for proof.
